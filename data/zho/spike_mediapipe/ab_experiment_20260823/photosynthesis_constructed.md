Plants make their own food using sunlight. This process is called photosynthesis. A plant
uses sunlight, water, and a gas from the air called carbon dioxide to make food. This happens
mostly inside the leaves of the plant. Inside each leaf cell there is a green substance called
chlorophyll, and chlorophyll is what captures the light energy from the sun. The plant's roots
take in water from the soil, and the water travels up through the plant to reach the leaves.
During photosynthesis, the plant also releases a gas called oxygen into the air, which people
and animals need to breathe. Photosynthesis is how a plant grows and stays alive.
