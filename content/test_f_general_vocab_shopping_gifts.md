# A Trip to the Market

On the weekend, our family went shopping in the old market. My sister wanted
to visit the perfume and oud shop, because she loves the smell of incense and
oud wood. My brother bought chocolate and cake for a birthday celebration.

We also stopped to help an elderly man carry his bags -- being helpful and
active in the community is something we are taught from a young age. Before
we left, we took a photograph together in front of the market, and my father
gave everyone a small gift as an award for a fun day out.
