# Daily Habits and Character

Every morning we wake up and get ready for school. Some students find it easy to wake up early, while others feel weak and tired and struggle to get out of bed. A clever student uses a clear routine: wake up, read a book, and eat breakfast before school starts.

Patience is an important quality. A polite and patient student does not get bored during a difficult lesson. They stay active and quiet, listen carefully, and try not to forget what they have learned. If a student loses focus, they should not lose hope, with practice, even a difficult subject becomes easy.
