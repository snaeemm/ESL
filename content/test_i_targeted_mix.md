# A Family Day at School and Home

Father is a doctor, and mother is a teacher.

Brother and sister go to school every morning.

Today the weather is hot, and the sun is bright. Yesterday it was cold, and the rain was heavy.

After school, students play football and sing songs together.

The exam was successful, and the family is proud.

In the evening, the family visits grandmother in the city.

The house has a farm and a beautiful flower garden.
