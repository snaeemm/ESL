# A Day With My Family

My father is a doctor and my mother is a teacher. My sister is intelligent and my brother is patient. My uncle is an engineer, and my grandfather was a farmer.

Every morning we wake up early and walk to school together. At school, the teacher gives us a new book to read. My class is good, and my results are excellent.

Today the weather is hot, and the sun is bright. Yesterday it was cold, and the rain was heavy. My friend runs fast, but I am slow.

After school, we play football and sing songs together. My exam was successful, and my family is proud.

In the evening, we visit our grandmother in the city. Near her house there is a farm with a beautiful flower garden.
